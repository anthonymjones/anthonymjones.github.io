Privacy Policy

Jones Mail Sorter · Effective October 26, 2026

Who uses it

Jones Mail Sorter is a personal tool used only by Anthony Jones, with his own Google account. It has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads messages, labels and filters in his own Gmail account.

How the data is used

Only to sort and manage the owner's own mail: applying labels, archiving routine messages, managing filters and highlighting messages that need his attention.

Where it is processed

Mail is processed on the owner's own computer. Some message content may also be processed by AI services the owner uses personally.

Sharing

Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else.

Retention

Mail stays in the owner's Gmail account. The tool keeps only the working data it needs to sort mail, such as per-sender sorting rules.

Google API data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
